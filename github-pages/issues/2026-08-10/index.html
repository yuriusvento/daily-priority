01Cinema / AI

AI cinema gains its own festival infrastructure

Astana AI Film Festival is accepting films in which generative artificial intelligence plays a substantial role in production. By establishing dedicated rules, an international jury and significant funding, the festival treats AI-native cinema as a distinct competitive field rather than a technical extension of conventional filmmaking.

The total prize fund is $1 million. The thematic competition offers a $450,000 grand prize, while the open programme awards $50,000 each for directing, visual language, story, character and concept. Films must run for at least three minutes.

The festival’s official website lists August 31, 2026 as the updated submission deadline.

02Research

Knowledge diversity needs a connected collaboration network

A study by researchers at Illinois Institute of Technology and Tsinghua University examines the relationship between knowledge diversity, interaction structures and collective intelligence.

The analysis suggests that knowledge diversity does not produce an unconditional advantage. In fragmented networks it can correlate negatively with collective outcomes. Structural integration, by contrast, helps groups mobilise distributed knowledge and reduces the negative effects of disconnection.

The practical conclusion is clear: differences become a collective resource when the collaboration network allows knowledge to circulate and connect.

03Media / AI

AI-native content moves into continuous broadcasting

Fairground Entertainment has announced Fairground AI Creator TV, a free, ad-supported FAST channel devoted entirely to content produced with artificial intelligence.

According to the company, the channel is scheduled to launch in August across more than five connected-TV and FAST platforms. Its programming will feature independent creators and more than 40 AI studios.

The experiment moves AI production beyond isolated short-form videos and into continuous distribution. Its performance will test whether AI-native content can sustain a dedicated, round-the-clock media channel.

04Brazil / Flight

A major paragliding event approaches in Andradas

The second stage of the 2026 Campeonato Paranaense de Parapente is scheduled for August 15–16 in Andradas, Minas Gerais.

The event page maintained by the Brazilian Free Flight Confederation lists a limit of 125 trackers and indicates that confirmed participation is close to capacity. The location is internationally known in the free-flight community for the Pico do Gavião launch.

The event continues Brazil’s busy competition season and brings together pilots from several regions of the country.

05Space

August skies: two eclipses, the Perseids and bright Venus

NASA highlights four major astronomical events in August. On August 12, the path of a total solar eclipse will cross northern Russia, Greenland, Iceland and northern Spain, with a small section of Portugal also affected.

The Perseid meteor shower peaks on the night of August 12–13 under a new moon, creating especially dark viewing conditions. From August 14 to 16, Venus will appear prominently low in the western sky after sunset.

On August 27–28, a deep partial lunar eclipse will be visible across much of North and South America and parts of Europe and Africa.
